Payment Issues Alert

Catch every unsuccessful premium payment. Remind on Day 3 and Day 7, in English, Malay or Chinese.

1. Load your ePartner policy list

ePartner → Applications → Online Enquiry → View All Policies → export to Excel or CSV.

🔒 Your file is read on this computer only. Nothing is uploaded and no message is sent without you pressing Send.

2. Check the columns

Settings & message wording

Placeholders: {name} {policy} {amount} {date} {agent}. Your name and phone are added at the end automatically.

How to use (2 minutes a day)
  1. In ePartner, export View All Policies (Excel or CSV) and load it with Choose file.
  2. First time only: open Check the columns and make sure each item points to the right column. Tick which payment statuses mean "unsuccessful".
  3. Open Send today. For each customer, read the message, change the language if needed, then press Open in WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp opens with the message ready, and you press Send there.
  4. Come back and press Confirm sent. That's how the app knows not to remind the same customer twice.
  5. Needs checking lists rows the app won't message because something is missing or unclear, such as no mobile number, a landline, an unreadable date or a duplicate policy. Fix them in the file and reload.
  6. When a customer pays, press Mark paid. If the payment is still unsuccessful 3 days after the Day-7 reminder, the customer moves to Call personally.

Rules: Day-3 reminder when the payment has been unsuccessful for 3 days, then a Day-7 reminder if it's still unsuccessful after 7 days. Days are counted from the unsuccessful payment date in your file. History is saved in this browser on this computer, so use the same browser each day.
