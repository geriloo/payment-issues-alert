# Payment Issues Alert

Catch every unsuccessful premium payment and follow up on time.

- Load your ePartner **View All Policies** export (Excel or CSV).
- The app lists who needs a **Day-3** or **Day-7** WhatsApp reminder today, in English, Bahasa Melayu or 中文.
- Press **Open in WhatsApp**, check the message, press Send, then **Confirm sent**.
- Rows with a missing or unclear mobile number, date or status are held back under **Needs checking**. No message is guessed.

**Privacy:** your policy file is read inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to this website or anywhere else. Your reminder history is stored only in the browser on your device.

**Install:** open the app in Chrome or Edge and press **Install app** (or browser menu → *Install*). On iPhone, use Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.

Message wording is a draft. Check it against your company's approved customer-communication guidelines before use.
